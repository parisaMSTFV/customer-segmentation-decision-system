# Decision Playbook

The segment names summarize relative holdout profiles; they are not personal attributes or permanent customer identities. Every proposed action is an experiment hypothesis.

| Segment | Decision objective | Measurement requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Loyal high value | Protect retention and margin | Incremental retention or margin with an untreated holdout |
| High value at risk | Diagnose and recover inactivity | Incremental reactivation, contact pressure, and unsubscribe rate |
| Growth potential | Increase profitable category depth | Incremental margin and repeat behavior, not conversion alone |
| Engaged low conversion | Remove funnel friction | Step-level conversion with a pre-registered experiment |
| Discount-led frequent | Improve unit economics safely | Contribution margin after subsidy and cannibalization |
| Dormant low value | Control reacquisition cost | Incremental response relative to low-cost suppression or holdout |

These actions are deliberately absent from the model objective. Clustering describes behavioral structure; it cannot prove which treatment will work.
